🎯 Executive Summary for Ankit

Our recruitment funnel does not have an inbound volume problem. Rather, it suffers from heavy bandwidth absorption by low-yield channels and severe drop-off at the interview stage:

  • Effort Drain: Job Boards (4.2% conv) and LinkedIn (2.6% conv) consumed 71.9% of all interview loops (115/160) for only 11 hires combined.
  • Under-invested Star: Employee Referrals yielded 41.2% hire conversion at just 2.0 interviews per hire with 100% offer acceptance.
  • Pending Offer Mirage: 5 offers remain pending for 47 to 283 days ($667k total committed comp). When properly treated as lost, true operational acceptance is 72.2% (not 83.9%).
  • Headcount Starvation: Critical business departments are severely under-resourced: Data & Analytics (0% fill), Engineering (20% fill), Marketing (0% fill).
